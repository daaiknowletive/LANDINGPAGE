Mark Tampering & Unaudited Changes

Unprotected spreadsheets and manual report cards allow unverified grade changes without audit trails, destroying parent trust during final evaluations.

Slow Roll Call & Delayed Alerts

Paper registers eat up 15 minutes of every morning class. Parents only find out about unauthorized student absences days later after it is already too late.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions by School Leaders

Clear, non-technical answers directly from our official Institute Administration Guide.

What if a teacher or staff member forgets their password?
The Institute Admin can navigate to the Staff Management directory, click on the staff member's profile, and select 'Reset Password' or 'Send Login Link'. This generates a secure temporary login link delivered directly to the staff member's registered mobile number or email address.
Can teachers see what fees a student owes?
Absolutely not. Knowletive enforces strict role-based data segregation. The Fee Management and Accounts modules are visible exclusively to the Institute Admin and designated Accountants. Teachers only see student names, roll numbers, attendance records, and academic marks.
Does the system work on a smartphone, or do teachers need laptops?
Knowletive is 100% responsive and optimized for mobile devices. Teachers can take daily roll calls, enter exam marks, and check timetables directly on their personal smartphones via any standard mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) without downloading heavy applications.
What happens if a student pays fees partially in installments?
You can easily log partial payments in Module 3 (Fee Management). Enter the specific amount received under Cash, Cheque, UPI, or Bank Transfer. The software automatically issues an itemized receipt for the paid amount, updates the student ledger, and rolls over the pending balance.
How do we handle annual student promotions at the end of the academic year?
Use the End-of-Year Batch Transition wizard in Settings. Simply select the current class (e.g., Grade 5-A), filter eligible passing students, and click 'Promote to Grade 6-A'. The system preserves all historical attendance and fee records while establishing the fresh academic year ledger.
Can parents see other students' attendance or exam marks?
No. Each parent logs into a private, encrypted portal linked specifically to their registered mobile number. Parents can only view their own children's daily attendance records, itemized fee receipts, exam report cards, and school announcements.
