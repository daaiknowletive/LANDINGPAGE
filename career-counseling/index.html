Frequently Asked Questions

Who conducts the 1-on-1 career counseling session?
Sessions are conducted exclusively by senior academic and corporate mentors with 15+ years of real-world hiring, engineering, and admission counseling experience in Pune.
Can parents participate in the counseling session?
Yes, highly encouraged! Parent participation ensures everyone is aligned on the student's natural aptitude, financial considerations, and realistic academic expectations without family conflict.
What is the difference between Baner offline and online sessions?
The deliverables, psychometric evaluation, and 1-on-1 advisor depth are 100% identical. Offline sessions take place at our Baner, Pune center, while online sessions use screen-shared video calls for outstation families.
At what stage is career counseling most beneficial?
It is most crucial at key transition milestones: Class 10 (stream selection), Class 12 (degree & CET entrance exam focus), college 3rd/final year (job domain specialization), and early career professionals planning a role transition.
Does Knowletive guarantee college admissions or job placements?
No ethical firm can guarantee admission, as college seats are strictly governed by merit cutoffs and computerized CAP rounds. We empower you with data, strategy, and roadmap execution to maximize your probability of success.
