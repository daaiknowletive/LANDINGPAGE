Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Maharashtra CET exams do you provide admission guidance for?
We provide comprehensive counseling for MHT-CET Engineering (B.Tech), MHT-CET Pharmacy (B.Pharm), MAH-MBA / MMS CET, and MAH-MCA CET across all affiliated colleges in Maharashtra.
Do you guarantee admission or management quota seats?
No. We strictly adhere to State CET Cell Maharashtra merit-based rules. Computerized CAP round allotment is strictly governed by rank, reservation, and option preferences. We ensure your preference order is mathematically optimized to secure the highest-ranking college you merit.
When should we start the CET admission counseling process?
The ideal time is immediately after the CET exam or upon percentile declaration. This provides ample time to verify and correct category certificates (caste validity, non-creamy layer, income certificates) before official CAP registration begins.
Can parents attend the option form strategy session?
Yes, parents are strongly encouraged to participate. Engineering and MBA admissions involve crucial financial, geographical, and hostel decisions that the entire family should align on.
What is the difference between Auto-Freeze and Betterment (Float)?
If you are allotted your #1 choice in CAP Round 1, it is automatically frozen and you must report to that college. If you get a lower choice, you can opt for Betterment: this preserves your current seat while allowing you to compete for higher-preference colleges in subsequent rounds.
